Regenerating
============

The files are exactly what Google returns for the URL below. Fetch it with a
current desktop browser user-agent (an old or absent UA gets ttf instead of
woff2), then save every url() in the response and keep the naming scheme above.

Adding an icon means adding its name to icon_names here AND re-downloading,
or the new icon renders as its literal ligature text -- the subset will not
contain the glyph.

https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Exo+2:ital,wght@0,300;0,400;0,500;0,600;0,700;1,400&family=Playfair+Display:ital,wght@0,700;0,900;1,700&family=Material+Symbols+Rounded:opsz,wght,FILL,GRAD@20..48,100..700,0..1,-50..200&icon_names=accessibility_new,add,album,arrow_back,bug_report,build_circle,center_focus_strong,chevron_left,chevron_right,close,code,computer,copyright,dark_mode,electric_bolt,emoji_events,fit_screen,flight,fullscreen,fullscreen_exit,gavel,handyman,hub,library_music,light_mode,mail,memory,military_tech,open_in_full,open_in_new,pause,person,photo_camera,photo_library,play_arrow,play_circle,progress_activity,radio_button_checked,refresh,remove,search,settings,speaker,stars,sync,thermostat,upload_file,videocam_off,visibility,visibility_off,zoom_in&display=swap

The @font-face rules that point at these files live in src/styles/index.css.
